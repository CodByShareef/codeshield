Authorized use only

You may use CodeShield only for websites, source code and files that you own or are expressly authorized to inspect. You are responsible for the target, the data you submit and compliance with applicable law, contracts and provider terms.

Prohibited use

  • No unauthorized scanning, credential testing, account enumeration, CAPTCHA bypass or evasion.
