Interpret results carefully

CodeShield reports patterns and response metadata visible in publicly accessible source. A finding may be a false positive, and a clean result does not prove that a website is secure.

What the tool can and cannot see

  • It can inspect delivered HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON, linked assets and selected response headers.
  • It cannot reliably assess server-side authorization, databases, secrets that are not delivered, private routes, authenticated flows or runtime behavior behind user actions.
  • Source maps, third-party scripts and client-side storage may require manual review even when no high-severity finding appears.

Safe interpretation

Confirm findings manually, rotate exposed credentials immediately, review deployment history and test fixes in an authorized environment. Do not treat this tool as permission to test a third-party system.
